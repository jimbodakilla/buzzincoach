Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version
  • A parent or coach creates the account. Students only need a first name or nickname, typed in by the parent.
  • We use what we collect to run the training and show progress to the student and the parent. That's it.
  • No ads, no tracking tools, no selling or renting data, ever.
  • Students can't chat, message, post or share anything inside BuzzIn Coach.
  • Parents can see, change or delete their student's information at any time by writing to support@buzzincoach.com.

  • We don't use student information to build marketing profiles.

Children's privacy: our notice to parents

BuzzIn Coach is made for students in grades 5 to 8, many of them under 13. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

How a parent gives consent

Only an adult can create an account. The parent or coach signs in with their own email and adds each student. Before students can play, the parent starts a membership with a payment card on Stripe's secure page, after reading this notice. By adding a student and starting the membership, the parent consents to the collection and use described here.

What we collect from children and how we use it

From students we collect only the practice activity listed above (answers, scores, buzz timing, progress and settings), the device label and a sign-in token. Their first name or nickname is typed in by the parent. We use it only to run the training and show progress to the student and their parent or coach. We do not require a student to give more information than the activity needs.

Disclosure

We don't disclose children's information to third parties except the service providers listed above, which act only on our behalf. We do not allow children to make their information public.

A parent's rights

A parent can, at any time:

  • review their student's progress in the Parent dashboard, or ask us for a copy of everything we hold about the student;
  • correct the student's name, or ask us to;
  • stop any further collection by removing the student or ending the membership;
  • ask us to delete the student's information.

Write to support@buzzincoach.com from the account's email address. We confirm the request comes from the account holder before acting, and we complete it within 30 days.

Schools and coaches

When a school or coach creates the account, the school or coach gives consent on behalf of parents for use in an educational setting, as COPPA allows, and is responsible for any notice to parents that its own policies require. Parents of students on a school or team account can contact us directly with any request.

How long we keep information

  • While a membership is active, we keep the account and each student's progress so the training can continue.
  • When a parent removes a student or asks us to delete it, we delete that student's information within 30 days.
  • If a membership ends and nobody signs in for 12 months, we delete the account and all student information.
  • We keep payment records only as long as tax and accounting laws require. These don't include student information.
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule, so deleted information also leaves our backups.

How we protect information

All traffic uses encryption (HTTPS). Our database lets each family see only its own information, and each student plays on one signed-in device at a time. Access to our systems is limited to the people who run BuzzIn Coach, and we review our safeguards regularly. No system is perfectly secure, but if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you promptly.

Information kept on your device

BuzzIn Coach saves your sign-in and a copy of the student's practice progress in the browser's local storage so the app works smoothly. We don't use advertising cookies. Signing out or switching students clears the student's copy from the device.

Your choices

You can update your email, payment card or plan, or cancel, in Manage membership and billing in the Parent dashboard. To ask for a copy of your information or to delete your account, write to support@buzzincoach.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. We honor those requests for everyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use children's information, we'll email the parent first and ask for new consent where the law requires it.
