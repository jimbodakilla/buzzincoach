BuzzIn Coach paces new questions and levels on purpose: memory locks in between short sessions, and missed questions come back until they're mastered. Parents and coaches can set the number of daily levels. To keep the training effective and the service fair, there is a daily limit on new questions per student. We may adjust features, modes and limits as we improve the training.
